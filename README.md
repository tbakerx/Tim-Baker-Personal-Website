## 🎯 Research Importance

The comprehensive data on psoriasis research are numerous and complex, making it difficult to retrieve and classify manually. The ability to quickly mine literature based on various fine topics using deep learning natural language processing technology to assess research topics and trends in the field of psoriasis disease will have a significant impact on:

- 🔬 **Medical Researchers**: Identifying emerging research hotspots and trends
- 👨‍⚕️ **Clinicians**: Staying updated with the latest developments in psoriasis treatment
- 👥 **Patients**: Accessing reliable health education materials
- 📊 **Healthcare Policy Makers**: Understanding research priorities and gaps

---

## 🚀 Interactive Neural Topic Model Visualization

The neural topic model based on deep learning and interactive web tools can effectively identify the research hotspots and trends in psoriasis literature, which can assist clinicians and patients in conveniently retrieving and comparing pertinent topics and research accomplishments of various years.

### Key Features:
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Analysis**: Autotuned topic modeling with intelligent agents
- 📈 **Trend Identification**: Track research evolution over time
- 🎨 **Interactive Visualization**: Explore topics dynamically with Tableau
- 🔍 **Comprehensive Coverage**: Analysis of extensive psoriasis literature database
